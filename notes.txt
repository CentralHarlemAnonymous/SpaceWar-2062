Your requirements describe a faithful and flexible modern adaptation of SpaceWar for iOS, making use of SpriteKit and MultipeerConnectivity. Here’s an assessment and summary of how you can approach the implementation for each major feature, along with validation that this is all achievable with your current tech choices:

⸻

1. Two Spaceships (Distinct Shapes)
• SpriteKit is perfect for rendering ships with custom shapes.
• You can draw vector-style ships using SKShapeNode or pre-rendered line art, easily supporting a “narrow with a bulb” and “dart”-shaped ship.

⸻

2. Movement: Rotation, Thrust, Coast
• SpriteKit physics (SKPhysicsBody) allows you to rotate, apply thrust (impulse or force), and let ships coast naturally with inertia.
• You can handle player input (touch or onscreen buttons) to trigger these transformations.

⸻

3. Edge Behavior: User-Selectable
• SpriteKit allows custom handling at screen edges:
   • Wrap: Instantly reposition to opposite edge when leaving the screen.
   • Bounce: Invert velocity when hitting the edge.
   • Selective: E.g., bullets disappear but ships bounce—logic can be set per object type, based on user preferences.

⸻

4. Gravity (Inverse r², Optional)
• You can simulate a central gravity well by applying a force proportional to 1/r² from a central point (the “star”) on each frame to ships and optionally projectiles.
• This can be toggled in user settings.

⸻

5. Shooting: Missiles, Straight or Curved
• Ships can fire projectiles using SKNode or SKShapeNode. Projectiles can travel straight, or you can optionally apply gravity to their physics bodies.

⸻

6. Damage, Collisions, Lives
• Use SpriteKit’s physics collision system:
   • Detect collisions between ships, projectiles, and the central star.
   • Destroy ships appropriately and manage lives.

⸻

7. Objective
• All the above come together to create the gameplay loop: outmaneuver, shoot, and avoid hazards.

⸻

8. Solo (AI) or Networked Multiplayer
• Against Computer: Implement basic AI for maneuvering and attacking.
• Against Another Player: Use MultipeerConnectivity to synchronize player input and game state across devices. You’re already set up for this with your MultipeerManager.

⸻

9. Visual Style (Pseudo-Vector, Black, Bright Touchpoints)
• SpriteKit allows black backgrounds and line-based ship/stage graphics.
• For “brighter” intersections: overlay or double up line segments where they cross/touch, or use blending modes to simulate brightness.
• Consistent with the mainframe aesthetic.

⸻

10. iPhone & iPad Support
• SpriteKit scenes adapt to device size. Use safe area guides and anchor points for layout.

⸻

Conclusion:
Everything you describe is very doable with SpriteKit for graphics/physics and MultipeerConnectivity for local multiplayer. You can layer in user preferences and AI for a complete, modern SpaceWar experience.
